The Biggest Takeaway Across 2+ Years of Coding

In the beginning, every compiler error and runtime exception felt like a personal failure or proof that I wasn't cut out for computer science. Today, I view every bug as a conversation with the machine. The computer is never being malicious—it is simply executing exactly what I asked it to do, with zero assumptions. Learning to listen to that feedback without emotion is the superpower that makes a real programmer.
